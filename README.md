# Public Speaking Checkpoint

**Theme:** *Do social media networks strengthen or degrade human relationships?*

**Position defended:** **social media networks STRENGTHEN human relationships.**

The presentation is available in this repository:

- [`Social-Media-and-Human-Relationships.pptx`](./Social-Media-and-Human-Relationships.pptx) — 18-slide PowerPoint presentation.

## Argument summary

### Factual arguments
1. **Scale** — 5.2 billion people use social media worldwide (≈64% of the global
   population, DataReportal 2025). Never before have so many people been able to
   connect.
2. **Distance** — 281 million international migrants (UN, 2020) can keep a daily
   link with their family thanks to messaging and video calls.
3. **Communities** — 1.8 billion people use Facebook groups every month for
   support, health, education and mutual aid.

### Emotional arguments
1. **Presence** — a daily video call keeps grandparents inside the life of a
   child who lives far away.
2. **Belonging** — support groups give a voice to people who thought they were
   alone (anxiety, rare diseases, grief).
3. **Shared emotion** — joy, empathy and solidarity are shared instantly at
   global scale.

### Rational arguments
1. **Cost & speed** — the marginal cost of a message is almost zero, so weak
   ties survive and create opportunities (Granovetter, 1973).
2. **Inclusion** — asynchronous communication adapts to schedules, disabilities
   and time zones.
3. **Social capital** — active use is correlated with greater bridging and
   bonding social capital (Ellison et al., 2007; Pew, 2011).
